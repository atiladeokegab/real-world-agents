## Initial request

I can see a £3.20 fee beside a foreign-currency cash withdrawal in my H&V secure portal, but I don't understand what the charge is for or how its amount was determined. Please check the fee against the tariff that applied to that transaction.

## Overview

Mara Ellery queried a £3.20 fee shown against a foreign-currency cash withdrawal. After the request was escalated under case HV-034, an H&V agent compared the transaction record with the tariff applicable on its processing date. H&V sent a secure explanation identifying the fee basis and confirming the amount matched the applicable tariff entry. No refund decision was made.

## Next action at escalation

Compare the recorded transaction type with the H&V tariff in force on the transaction's processing date.

## Outcome

H&V explained that the £3.20 debit was the fee for the recorded foreign-currency cash withdrawal, applied under the tariff entry in force on the processing date, and confirmed the amount matched that entry. Whether any refund is due remains undecided.
