# Case 017: The Standing Order That Did Not Run

## Case details

## Initial request

I need to know whether my monthly standing order for rent was sent and, if it was not, why. The recipient says they have not received it.

## Overview

Mara Ellery contacted Harrow & Vale Bank after the recipient of her monthly rent standing order said no payment was visible. She asked the bank to check the specific scheduled run. The agent checked the standing-order journal, and the secure portal displayed that it was not executed because there were insufficient cleared funds at the scheduled processing time.

## Next action at escalation

Check the journal entry for Mara’s specific scheduled standing-order run and establish its recorded status and reason.

## Outcome

The secure portal shows “Not executed — insufficient cleared funds at the scheduled processing time.” H&V’s records show that no payment was made for that run; the customer has not been advised to pay again without first checking for any new or pending activity.
