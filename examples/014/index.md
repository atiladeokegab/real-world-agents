## Initial request

I saw a Direct Debit collection to Northmere Water Services on my March statement, but I have not seen another one. Please tell me whether the mandate is currently active; I am not asking you to cancel it or assess what I owe the organisation.

## Overview

Mara Ellery, the account holder, asked H&V to check the bank-record status of her Direct Debit mandate after seeing a historic collection on her March 2025 statement. The agent checked the mandate record and H&V displayed its current status in the secure portal. This confirms the bank-side status only; it does not establish any obligation to Northmere Water Services.

## Next action at escalation

The agent's first action was to request a current status check from H&V's mandate journal, without making any change to the mandate.

## Outcome

The secure portal shows the Northmere Water Services Direct Debit mandate as Active, checked on 14 August 2025. Any obligation to the organisation remains unassessed; the absence of newer collections does not by itself mean the mandate was cancelled.
