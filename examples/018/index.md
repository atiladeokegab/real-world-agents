## Overview

Mara Ellison contacted H&V after the secure portal showed two future payment lines to the same payee. The agent checked H&V's instruction journal and confirmed the entries arose from separate instructions: a standing order and a direct debit mandate. Neither was cancelled because the customer had not specified which instruction to change.

## Next action at escalation

Check the types and internal references of both instructions in H&V's journal before considering any change.

## Outcome

H&V confirmed that the two future entries have separate instruction records: one standing order and one direct debit mandate. Neither was cancelled; the customer must identify which instruction, if any, they want changed.
