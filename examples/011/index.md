## Initial request

I need to know the date my active monthly standing order is next due to run. I have forgotten its schedule and want to plan my household budget.

## Overview

Mara Ellison escalated through secure chat after seeing in the secure portal that her standing order was active but not recalling its next run date. The agent checked the bank's standing-order record and relayed the date shown there.

## Next action at escalation

The agent's first action at escalation is to ask H&V's standing-order journal to verify the next scheduled run date.

## Outcome

H&V's record shows the next run date as 3 September 2025, and that date was shown to Mara in the secure portal. The future payment has not been executed, and the scheduled date does not guarantee when funds will reach the recipient.
