## Initial request

Rowan wants to know why their H&V card became temporarily unavailable after it was declined during a grocery purchase, and whether the restriction has been lifted.

## Overview

Rowan contacted H&V through secure chat after seeing the card-unavailable status in the secure portal. The agent checked the permitted explanation and disclosure limit in the card journal. H&V confirmed that the permitted category was a temporary security-related restriction; no lifting of the restriction was confirmed.

## Next action at escalation

Check the card journal for the permitted restriction category and the limit on what can be disclosed.

## Outcome

H&V provided the permitted category: a temporary security-related restriction. The restriction's exact basis was not shown, and no release or timing was promised.
